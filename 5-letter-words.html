What are 5-letter words?

5-letter words are five-character English terms containing 15,921 legal entries in standard word-game dictionaries. They form the foundational lexicon for popular daily puzzle games like Wordle, Scrabble, and Words With Friends due to their ideal balance of vowel distribution and consonant stem patterns.

Best 5-Letter Words for Wordle

Starting words in Wordle dictate how quickly you narrow down unknown letters. Top strategic openers backed by information theory and puzzle algorithms include:

  • CRANE: Analyzed by data scientists as one of the single most efficient openers for uncovering R, N, E.
  • SLATE: Recommended by NYT WordleBot for testing top-tier consonants S, L, T paired with A and E.
  • ADIEU & AUDIO: Fan-favorite vowel-heavy openers testing four vowels in a single guess.
  • RAISE: Excellent for testing early S and R positions alongside three essential vowels.
