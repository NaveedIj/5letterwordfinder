8 Letter Words

Complete 8-Letter Word Lexicon & Advanced Scrabble Strategy

There are 51,627 eight-letter words in the official English dictionary. Eight-letter words are among the most strategic plays in competitive word games like Scrabble and Words With Friends. Connecting all 7 rack tiles to an existing board tile yields an 8-letter "bingo" play and a massive 50-point bonus.

Strategic Value of 8-Letter Words

While 7-letter words use your entire rack, 8-letter words allow players to hook all 7 tiles onto a single letter already on the board. Learning common 8-letter prefixes (like UN-, RE-, MIS-, DIS-) and suffixes (like -ING, -NESS, -ABLE, -MENT) helps you transform 7-letter rack combinations into devastating 8-letter plays.
