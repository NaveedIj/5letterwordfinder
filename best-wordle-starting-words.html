Best Wordle Starting Words (Ranked by Data)

Data-Driven Wordle Strategy & Analysis

Based on analysis of 15,921 five-letter words and published data-science research, the best Wordle starting words are CRANE, SLATE, and TRACE. They test the most common letters (A, E, S, R, T) in their most common positions. The differences between the top picks are tiny — picking any one of them and using it daily beats switching randomly.

What Makes a Good Starting Word?

A great Wordle opener follows three rules:

  1. Five unique letters. Never repeat a letter — a word like SPEED only tests four unique letters, wasting a slot.
  2. Two or three vowels. Vowels (A, E, I, O, U) appear in almost every answer, but don't overdo it (see the ADIEU trap below).
  3. Common consonants. R, S, T, L, and N carry the most information. Our analysis of 15,921 words confirms these dominate.

Avoid plurals (Wordle never picks them as answers) and the letter J — it appears in the fewest answers.

The ADIEU Trap (Why Vowel-Heavy Starters Lose)

ADIEU is the most popular starting word in Wordle — and the least efficient among the top 30 starters, according to NYT data from 515M+ games. Players using ADIEU need roughly 74 more guesses per year than SLATE players.

Why? Four vowels feel productive, but consonants (R, S, T, L, N) carry more information per guess. AUDIO has the same problem. Use a balanced starter instead — or play ADIEU first and follow with a consonant-heavy second guess using our AI Word Solver.

Should You Use the Same Starter Every Day?

For maximum score: yes. Pick one top-tier word (CRANE, SLATE, or TRACE) and use it daily — consistency lets you read the feedback patterns faster.

For fun: rotate. Many players enjoy varying their opener. You lose a fraction of efficiency but gain variety. There's no wrong answer — Wordle is a game, not an exam.
