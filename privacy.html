Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. Learn how we handle your data (hint: we don't).

Our Privacy Philosophy

At 5 Letter Word Finder, we believe that your data belongs to you. Unlike many other tools on the web, we have built our word finder to be completely "client-side." This means all the logic, word lists, and searches happen entirely within your own web browser.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any of the following:

  • Your Search Queries: The letters you type and the words you find are never sent to our servers.
  • Personal Information: We do not require accounts, names, or email addresses to use our tool.
  • Browsing History: We do not track your activity across other websites.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage for exactly one thing: remembering your preference for Dark Mode. This ensures that the site looks the way you want it to every time you return. This data stays on your device and is never shared.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Fonts to provide high-quality typography. This service may collect basic technical information like your IP address to serve the font files correctly. We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising trackers that compromise your privacy.

Updates to This Policy

If we ever change how this site works in a way that impacts your privacy, we will update this page immediately. However, our commitment to private, local-first computing will remain at the core of what we do.
