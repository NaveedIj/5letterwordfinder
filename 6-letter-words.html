6 Letter Words

Complete 6-Letter Word Lexicon & Analysis

There are 29,874 six-letter words in the official English dictionary. Six-letter words form the backbone of popular word games like Wordle, Scrabble, and Wordle variants. Learning high-scoring six-letter words and common letter patterns significantly improves your win rate in competitive word puzzles.

Why 6-Letter Words Matter in Word Games

Six-letter words represent a critical strategic tier in word games. In Scrabble, playing a 6-letter word leaves only one tile on your rack, setting up easy 7-letter "bingo" plays on your next turn. In Wordle-style games with expanded word lengths, 6-letter words demand deeper pattern recognition than standard 5-letter puzzles.

Best 6-Letter Starting Words for Wordle Puzzles

If you're playing 6-letter Wordle variants, starting with high-probability letter combinations is crucial. Strong opening guesses test common vowels (E, A, O) and frequent consonants (R, S, T, N, L):

  • REASTS: Excellent coverage of R, E, A, S, T with common 6-letter ending -STS.
  • STAREN: Tests prime consonants S, T, R, N along with vowels A and E.
  • ORATES: Covers three vowels (O, A, E) and top consonants (R, T, S).
  • ARISEN: Vowel-heavy starter ideal for discovering middle positions.
