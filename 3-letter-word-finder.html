Highest-Scoring 3-Letter Scrabble Words

Three-letter words win Scrabble games. They let you dump awkward tiles like Z, X, and Q while scoring big — especially on premium squares. These are the highest-scoring 3-letter words in Scrabble, by face value:

  • ZUZ – 21 points (Z=10, U=1, Z=10): the highest-scoring 3-letter word in the game.
  • ZAX – 19 points: a tool for cutting (and for cutting down your opponent's lead).
  • ZEK – 16 points: an Ethiopian title.
  • FEZ, FIZ, WIZ, PYX – 15 points each: all use the Z or X to spike your score.
  • QAT, QIS – 12 points each: no U required, perfect for dumping the Q.

Play ZAX on a triple-letter square and that Z alone scores 30 — before the word multiplier. That's why competitive players memorize these.

Q-Without-U Words and X/Z Starters

When dealing with tricky tiles, check words starting with Q. The Q is the most feared tile in Scrabble — unless you know the Q-without-U words: QAT (a shrub), QIS (plural of qi), QIN (a Chinese dynasty), QUA (as in "qua"), and SUQ (a marketplace). Memorize these five and the Q becomes an opportunity, not a curse.

Short on vowels? X and Z openers save you: XED and XIS start with X, while ZAX, ZEK, and ZAP start with Z. Type any of these patterns into the finder above to discover more.

Mastering Scrabble Hooks

Three-letter words are the 'connectors' of the Scrabble board. Using high-value tiles like X, Q, and Z in short words can net you massive points without opening up the board to your opponent. For example, ZAX is a tournament favorite that effectively uses the Z hook.

Hooks are letters you attach to existing words to form new ones — and 3-letter words are the best hooks in Scrabble. The X is the king of hooks: add it to "AX" openings or close words on triple squares for ~70-point turns. Use the Excluded filter above to remove letters you've already played and find fresh hooks from your rack. Looking to build longer connections? Check out our 4-letter word finder.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best 3-letter words for Scrabble?

ZUZ (21 points), ZAX (19), and ZEK (16) are the highest-scoring. For dumping difficult tiles, learn QAT, QIS, FEZ, and PYX.

What is the highest-scoring 3-letter word in Scrabble?

ZUZ at 21 points (Z=10, U=1, Z=10). On premium squares it scores far more — a Z on triple-letter alone is worth 30.

What 3-letter words contain Q without U?

QAT, QIS, QIN, QUA, and SUQ are all Scrabble-legal words with Q and no U. QI (11 points) is the shortest.

What 3-letter words start with X or Z?

XED and XIS start with X; ZAX, ZEK, ZAP, and ZOO start with Z. These are lifesavers when your rack is all consonants.

How many 3-letter words are there in Scrabble?

More than 1,000 three-letter words are legal in Scrabble, which is why they're the backbone of board strategy — there's always a play.

How do 3-letter words help in Wordle?

They help you test and eliminate letters quickly. When few guesses remain, short patterns reveal which letters fit — enter your known letters in the finder above and watch the possibilities shrink.
