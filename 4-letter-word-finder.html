5. FAQ

Why do certain letter combinations appear more often?
Vowels act as the glue for consonants. Identifying pairs like "EA" or "OU" drastically reduces the number of valid outcomes.

Is there a faster way to handle difficult letters?
Yes. Prioritizing high-value consonants early, like J or Q, forces the solver to narrow down your choices instantly.
