7 Letter Words

Complete 7-Letter Word Lexicon & Bingo Strategy

There are 41,998 seven-letter words in the official English dictionary. Seven-letter words are famous in Scrabble and Words With Friends because playing all 7 tiles from your rack earns a 50-point "Bingo" bonus. Mastering 7-letter words is the fastest way to turn competitive word game matches in your favor.

The Power of the Scrabble 50-Point Bingo Bonus

🎯 What is a Bingo Play?

In standard Scrabble rules, if a player uses all 7 tiles on their rack in a single turn, they earn a 50-point bonus added to their turn score. Because a standard rack holds exactly 7 tiles, 7-letter words are the most frequent bingo plays in tournament word games.

Common 7-Letter Bingo Stems

Scrabble champions memorize 6-letter "stems" that easily combine with a 7th letter to form a valid 7-letter word. The most famous stem is TISANE (T-I-S-A-N-E):

  • TISANE + B = ABESTING, BANTIES
  • TISANE + C = CINEAST, ACETINS
  • TISANE + D = DESTAIN, SAINTED
  • TISANE + R = NASTIER, RETINAS
