5 Letter Fill in the Blank Word Finder

Search 5-Letter Words by Exact Position & Wildcards

A fill-in-the-blank 5-letter word finder lets you search with blanks (_) for unknown letters. Type known letters in their exact positions — like _ATCH, S____, or __A_E — and get every matching word instantly. Perfect for crosswords, Wordle green letters, and "words with these letters and a blank" puzzles. Free forever, no signup.

How Fill-in-the-Blank Search Works

Three steps:

  1. Type known letters in their exact 1st–5th positions
  2. Use _ (underscore) for every unknown letter
  3. Get results instantly — no button needed

Example: you know a crossword answer looks like _ATCH. Type it in — you get CATCH, MATCH, WATCH.

Worked Examples (Common Words First)

Pattern _ATCH — "ends with ATCH":

CATCH, MATCH, WATCH — plus HATCH, LATCH, PATCH.

Wordle note: none are common Wordle answers, but all are valid guesses.

Pattern S____ — "starts with S":

S is the most common starting letter (1,813 words in our data):

SALAD, SCALE, SCENE, SCORE, SENSE, SERVE, SEVEN, SHADE, SHAKE, SHALL — and top Wordle starters SLATE, STARE.

Wordle note: SLATE and SHAKE are excellent Wordle answers and openers.

Pattern __A_E — "A in 3rd, E in 5th":

AWAKE, AWARE, BLADE, BLAME, BRAVE, CHASE, FLAME, FRAME, GRADE, GRAVE

Wordle note: BRAVE, GRADE, and SHAKE are all strong Wordle answers.

Pattern _A___ — "A as 2nd letter":

The 2nd slot is vowel-heavy — A appears 2,871 times here:

BACON, BADGE, BAKER, BASIC, CABIN, CABLE, CANDY, CARRY, CATCH, CAUSE

Wordle note: CANDY and BAKER are common answers.

Pattern ___E_ — "E in 4th position":

E dominates the 4th slot (2,511 words):

AFTER, AGREE, ALIEN, ALTER, ANGEL, ANGER, ARMED, ASSET, BAKER, BRIEF

Pattern ____E — "ends with E":

The single most common ending (1,873 words):

ABOVE, ABUSE, ACUTE, AGREE, ALIKE, ALIVE, ALONE, ANGLE, ANKLE, APPLE

Wordle note: CRANE, SLATE, BRAVE, SHAKE — the best starters end in E.

Words with a Blank (Wildcard Tile)

A blank matches any letter. In our finder:

  • Leave the position empty (or type _) — it matches everything
  • Combine with known letters: _RA_E finds BRAVE, GRADE, CRANE

Scrabble blank strategy: blanks are worth 0 points but unlock bingos. Save them for 7-letter plays.

Position Search for Wordle (Greens)

Wordle green letters = correct position. This is exactly fill-in-the-blank search:

  1. Put green letters in their exact slots
  2. Add yellows to "wrong position"
  3. Add grays to "excluded"

Likely answers vs valid guesses: our 15,921-word list has every valid word, but Wordle answers come from a curated 2,656-word pool. Our finder shows both — common answers first.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find 5 letter words with A as the second letter?

Enter _A___ in a position finder. Common results: BAKER, CANDY, DANCE, PAPER, WATER, MAGIC, HAPPY.

What is a 5 letter fill-in-the-blank solver?

Type known letters with _ for blanks (e.g. _ATCH → CATCH, MATCH, WATCH). It returns every word matching the pattern.

How do I use a blank/wildcard in word search?

Leave the position empty or type _. It matches any letter — useful for Scrabble blank tiles and crossword gaps.

Can I search two positions at once, like 2nd letter A and ends with E?

Yes — fill both slots: _A__E gives WASTE, BASTE, PASTE, TASTE, HASTE. Our tool handles any combination of positions.
