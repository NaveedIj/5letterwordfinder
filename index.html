How to Use the 5 Letter Word Finder

Filter matching candidate words using exact tile positions, misplaced letter clues, and dataset options.

Content Reviewed Periodically • Word Game Datasets

1. Search & Pattern Filters

Our 5-letter word finder narrows down matching candidates in real time as you enter puzzle feedback:

  • Correct Position (Green): Enter letters whose exact 1st–5th slot position you already know.
  • Wrong Position (Yellow): Enter letters that exist in the word but belong in a different slot.
  • Excluded Letters (Gray): Enter confirmed incorrect letters to eliminate all matching words.
  • Must Include & Repeats: Filter for required letters regardless of slot, including double-letter occurrences (e.g., two E's for ABBEY).

2. Wordle Mode vs. Full Dictionary Mode

Select the underlying word list that matches your specific word game:

  • Wordle Mode: Filters against our 2,309-word list of common solution words used in daily Wordle puzzles.
  • Full Dictionary Mode: Searches our broader 15,921-word 5-letter list for Scrabble, anagrams, and crossword puzzles.

3. Strategic Opening Guesses

Choosing an opening 5-letter word with a useful mix of letters can help reveal clues and narrow the candidate list early:

  • Vowel-Oriented Openers: Words like AUDIO or ADIEU test multiple vowels in a single turn.
  • Consonant-Oriented Openers: Words such as CRANE, SLATE, or ALTER test multiple common consonants.
  • Common Word Endings: Words ending in E or Y represent common structural patterns (e.g., STARE, HEAVY).

4. Scrabble & Crossword Helper

Use the finder to solve board game tiles and crossword blanks:

  • Sort by Scrabble Values: Order candidate words from highest to lowest face value to maximize your turn points.
  • Crossword Blanks: Use green position slots to fill in missing letters when surrounding board tiles are placed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I filter 5-letter words with this tool?

Enter known letters in the correct position boxes (green), misplaced letters in the yellow row, and letters not in the word into the excluded box. The solver updates matching candidate words in real time as you type.

What is the difference between Wordle Mode and Full Dictionary Mode?

Wordle Mode filters across our 2,309-word curated daily solution list. Full Dictionary Mode searches our broader 15,921-word 5-letter list for Scrabble and crossword filtering.

How does the solver handle duplicate or repeated letters?

The filter engine tracks letter frequency automatically. If you enter duplicate letters into the input boxes or yellow slots, candidate words must contain at least that many letter occurrences.

Can I sort matching words by Scrabble score?

Yes. Use the order dropdown in the sidebar to sort results by Scrabble face values from highest to lowest or lowest to highest.

Are my search queries private?

Yes. Word matching and letter filtering run locally in your browser. Your letter inputs and search queries are not sent to our server.
